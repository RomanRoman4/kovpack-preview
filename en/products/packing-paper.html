Applications

For everything that needs wrapping, lining or keeping apart.

  • Wrapping goods

    Wraps products before packing and keeps surfaces free of scratches and dust.

  • Lining boxes

    Lines the box so the goods lie clean and protected.

  • Separating layers

    Keeps plates, panels and sheets apart so nothing rubs.

  • Lining shelves

    Lines shelves and compartments so goods stay clean and free of scratches.

Properties

  • Cut to size Cut from the roll to any size.

  • Formats to order Roll width and length to suit your goods.

  • Paper, not plastic film Replaces plastic film when wrapping goods.
